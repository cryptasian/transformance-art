Transformance Art is ...

Creative expression has been shown to reduce stress, build emotional resilience, and promote healing. But the benefits extend beyond the artist - research confirms that simply experiencing the arts can ease symptoms of depression and strengthen well-being. At Transformance Art, we empower artists to heal through their work and bring transformative arts experiences to the community.

Where Stories Find Expression

Transformance Art brings artists, individuals, and communities together through meaningful creative experiences—making space for stories that are difficult to put into words and fostering remembrance, healing, connection, and hope.

A Community Art Workshop

Kintsugi: The Art of Precious Scars

In Kintsugi, broken pottery is repaired with visible golden seams—honoring its history instead of hiding it.

Our guided workshops offer an approachable, hands-on space to explore resilience, healing, and the beauty that can emerge through care. No art experience is needed.

Signature Healing Arts Experiences

Meraki Projects

Interdisciplinary arts experiences created with soul, creativity, and love.

Each Meraki Project brings artists and communities together around a particular human experience—creating space for hope, healing, restoration, and meaningful conversation through music, movement, poetry, visual art, and storytelling.

Meraki [may-rah-kee]

To do something with soul, creativity, or love; to put something of yourself in your work.
