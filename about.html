Who We Are

Transformance Art is a multidisciplinary nonprofit arts organization bringing artists, individuals, and communities together through meaningful creative experiences. Through performances, remembrance events, retreats, collaborative projects, and community participation, we create space for stories and experiences that can be difficult to put into words, giving people an opportunity to reclaim dignity within the hard stories they carry. Rooted in faith and committed to artistic excellence, we work alongside churches and communities to foster remembrance, healing, connection, and hope.

Mission

Transformance Art bears witness to human stories through the arts by creating transformative experiences that foster connection, hope, and healing.

Vision

We envision communities where human stories are honored and the arts create pathways to expression, remembrance, healing, and transformation.

Transformance = Transformational + Performance

transformance (adj.)

A performance that has a transformational impact on the viewer; a performance that has been transformed from the way it is traditionally presented.
