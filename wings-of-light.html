What to Expect

Create a Keepsake

Guests may arrive beginning at 5:00 PM to create a free remembrance craft to take home.

Join the Wave of Light

At 7:00 PM, we will join families around the world in the International Wave of Light with a community candle-lighting ceremony.

Experience the Arts

The evening will include a women’s choir, poetry, visual art, and quiet moments of reflection.

Children Are Welcome

Children are warmly invited to participate at whatever level feels comfortable. The complimentary remembrance crafts are age-appropriate.

Come and Go Gently

You are welcome to arrive after 5:00 PM or leave quietly at any time. Activities have no strict start time; only the Wave of Light begins at a set time, 7:00 PM.

About the Project

Rooted in faith and open to all, Wings of Light offers a gentle space for families, friends, and members of our community to remember and honor babies lost through miscarriage, stillbirth, or during infancy. Whether your loss was recent or many years ago, you are invited to come as you are.

Through music, poetry, visual art, and quiet reflection, we will create space to remember, grieve, find hope, and honor the lasting bond between parents and their babies.

Transformance Art’s Meraki Projects use the arts to create space for healing, compassion, and meaningful conversation when words alone may not be enough. Whether you have personally experienced pregnancy or infant loss, are supporting someone who has, or simply wish to stand alongside families in our community, you are warmly invited to attend.

Share a Poem
